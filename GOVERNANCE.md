# Real Estate Savant Founder-Control Governance Charter

## Founder and creator

Ernest Rodriguez is the founder and creator of Real Estate Savant. Ernest is
the project's chief executive, product owner, and final decision-maker.

## Founder authority

Ernest retains final approval authority over the project, including:

- product strategy, branding, naming, and public positioning;
- application features, user experience, algorithms, scoring, and data use;
- releases, deployments, licensing, commercialization, and partnerships;
- repository administration, access control, credentials, and data sharing;
- hiring, contributor roles, budgets, and ownership-related proposals; and
- amendments to this charter.

No contributor, partner, automated agent, or AI system may override Ernest's
decisions or represent that it has independent authority to bind the project.
When instructions conflict or authority is uncertain, work must pause and be
referred to Ernest for a decision.

## Partner role

Jon is recognized as a minority partner and collaborator. Jon may contribute
code, data, analysis, and recommendations within access and responsibilities
delegated by Ernest. Those contributions do not independently authorize Jon to
change the product direction, transfer assets, grant access, enter agreements,
or make commitments on behalf of Real Estate Savant.

## Change and release control

Material changes should be made through a reviewed branch or pull request.
Changes affecting production, intellectual property, data access, security,
commercial terms, branding, or the Savant scoring methodology require Ernest's
explicit approval before release or external sharing.

## Legal status

This file is an internal project-governance and operating policy. It records
the founder-control intent for contributors and automated tools, but it is not
a substitute for signed legal documents. Equity percentages, entity ownership,
intellectual-property assignments, voting rights, fiduciary duties, and partner
exit terms must be established in attorney-reviewed agreements signed by the
relevant parties. This charter does not itself issue, transfer, or revoke
equity.

## Amendments

Only Ernest Rodriguez may approve an amendment to this charter. Proposed
changes may be drafted by others but do not take effect without Ernest's
explicit approval.
